# 01_LITERATUR — Akademik Kaynaklar

Bu klasör, BAHIS AGENT'ın bilimsel temelini oluşturan akademik makaleleri, kitapları ve özet notları içerir.

## Buraya Girecek İçerikler

### Temel Makaleler (öncelik sırası)
1. **Dixon & Coles (1997)** — "Modelling Association Football Scores and Inefficiencies in the Football Betting Market"
   - Futbol skor modellemenin temel makalesi
2. **Constantinou & Fenton (2012, 2013)** — pi-ratings, dynamic team strength
3. **Maher (1982)** — "Modelling association football scores" (Poisson temeli)
4. **Kelly (1956)** — "A New Interpretation of Information Rate" (Kelly Criterion)
5. **Thorp (2000)** — "The Kelly Criterion in Blackjack, Sports Betting, and the Stock Market"
6. **Levitt (2004)** — "Why are gambling markets organised so differently from financial markets?"
7. **Forrest, Goddard, Simmons (2005)** — "Odds-setters as forecasters: The case of English football"
8. **Štrumbelj (2014)** — Bahis piyasası verimliliği

### xG ve Modern Yaklaşımlar
- **Spearman (2018)** — "Beyond Expected Goals"
- **Eggels et al. (2016)** — "Expected goals in soccer: Explaining match results"
- **Rathke (2017)** — Possession-based xG

### Closing Line Value & Market Efficiency
- **Wunderlich & Memmert (2018)** — "Betting odds as a measure of forecasting accuracy"
- Pinnacle blog yazıları (CLV üzerine — akademik değil ama pratik)

### Kitaplar
- **Joseph Buchdahl** — "Squares & Sharps, Suckers & Sharks" (zorunlu)
- **Joseph Buchdahl** — "Monte Carlo or Bust"
- **Ed Miller & Matthew Davidow** — "The Logic of Sports Betting"
